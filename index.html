TradeSignal — Privacy Policy

For the TradeSignal browser extension · Last updated 18 September 2026

TradeSignal is a trade journal, portfolio tracker, watchlist and news reader that runs entirely in your browser.

We collect nothing. TradeSignal has no server, no account system, no analytics and no tracking. The developer cannot see your data.

What you create stays on your device

Your trades, notes, watchlist, price alerts, setups and settings are saved with the browser's extension storage on the computer where you installed the extension. They are never uploaded.

  • Settings sync between your own devices only if you have browser sync switched on. That is handled by your browser, not by us.
  • Removing the extension deletes this data. Use Dashboard → Import / export → Export JSON to keep a backup first.

Which servers the extension contacts

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
query1/query2.finance.yahoo.com The ticker symbols in your watchlist, open trades and searches Delayed prices, chart candles and symbol search
finance.yahoo.com, search.cnbc.com, feeds.content.dowjones.io, www.nasdaq.com, www.investing.com Nothing beyond the request itself Public RSS news headlines
s.tradingview.com The symbol you are charting Draws the chart widget. TradingView may set its own cookies inside that frame, under their own privacy policy
127.0.0.1 / localhost Nothing Optional: reads signals from open-source software you can run yourself. Only used if you run it

Requests carry the symbol you are looking at and ordinary browser request data, such as your IP address, which any website receives. No trades, notes or personal details are ever included.

These third parties have their own privacy policies: Yahoo · TradingView · CNBC · Dow Jones / MarketWatch · Nasdaq · Investing.com.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not read, modify or inject anything into the web pages you visit. Text is saved as a note only when you choose “Save … as a trade note” from the right-click menu.
  • It does not connect to any broker or trading platform, and it cannot place, change or cancel orders.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone, because it never collects any.

Permissions

storage (save your journal locally) · alarms (refresh prices on a timer) · notifications (price alerts and risk-limit warnings) · contextMenus (right-click to save a note) · commands (keyboard shortcuts), plus access to the data sources listed above.

Children

TradeSignal is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Any change to this policy will appear on this page with a new date, and in the extension's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please open an issue on GitHub.

TradeSignal is a personal research and journalling tool. It is not financial advice, and it never places trades.
